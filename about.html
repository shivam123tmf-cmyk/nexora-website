About

A serious tech brand, starting honest

Nexora is being built in India to make AI useful for businesses that cannot afford theatre.

Mission

Help local shops, SMEs, clinics, coaching centres, and service businesses solve real operational problems with affordable AI and automation.

Vision

Start with implementation services, earn trust with working systems, then grow repeatable products and SaaS — without pretending we are further along than we are.

The story so far

Nexora is a brand founded by Shivam Solanki, based in East Delhi. Shivam’s background includes running centre operations as Centre Head at NIIT Foundation Mandawali — coordinating youth programmes, partners, and day-to-day enrolment workflows. That floor-level experience shapes how we design automations: they have to survive busy evenings and incomplete data.

We are early. We do not publish fake client logos, invented metrics, or registration claims. When we have case studies, they will be real and named with permission.
