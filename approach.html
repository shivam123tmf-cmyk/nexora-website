Approach

Short pilots. Clear handoffs. No theatre.

We treat automation like an operations project: prove value on one workflow, then decide whether to expand.

Discover

Walk the floor or chat log. Pick one painful loop with a measurable outcome — hours, missed leads, or leakage.

Pilot

Ship a thin working demo in days using free or low-cost tools. You try it before any long commitment.

Automate

Harden the path that worked: WhatsApp, sheets, forms, alerts — documented and owned by your team.

Handoff

Training, runbooks, and a support window. If it is not understandable, it is not done.

  • We do not invent case studies, client logos, or performance metrics.
  • Pricing for early pilots is an estimate to test — confirmed only after scoping your workflow.
  • Tools stay pocket-friendly: prefer free tiers and open workflows over expensive lock-in.
