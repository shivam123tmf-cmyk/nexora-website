Approach

Prove value before you scale spend

Four steps. One painful workflow at a time. Stop early if the pilot does not help.

Discover

30–45 minute call or visit. We map who does what, where data lives (WhatsApp, paper, sheet), and what “done” looks like.

Pilot

A narrow build on free or low-cost tools. You try it on real messages or documents within days — not months.

Automate

Harden the flow: error handling, owner alerts, and the few integrations that actually matter.

Handoff

Written steps, access list, and a short training so your staff can run it without calling us every day.

Tools we prefer early

We favour stacks you can afford and leave: WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, n8n or similar automation, and mainstream AI APIs only when they clearly beat a simpler rule.

We do not train custom models on day one. Most businesses need reliable plumbing first.

PRE-FLIGHT

Your checklist before we build

  • One named workflow (not “do AI for us”)
  • Someone who owns the process daily
  • Willingness to try a 1–2 week pilot
  • Honest data: messy WhatsApp is fine
